# 👥 Gewerber Contributors

Thank you to everyone who contributes to Gewerber, an open‑core platform built to help solo Gewerbe owners and micro‑businesses thrive in Germany.

This document recognizes the people who help improve the project through code, documentation, design, localization, testing, and community support.

## 📏 Contribution Rules

To keep the project healthy:

- Follow the **[Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)**
- Follow the **[Security Policy](SECURITY.md)**
- Only contribute to open‑source modules
- Do not submit PRs for closed modules:
  - Banking
  - Tax/ELSTER
  - Employees
  - Subscriptions
  - AI assistant
  - Multi‑currency invoicing
  - Advanced accounting
