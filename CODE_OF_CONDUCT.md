# 📜 Gewerber Code of Conduct

Thank you for being part of the **Gewerber** community!
We are committed to creating a welcoming, respectful, and collaborative environment for everyone who uses, contributes to, or interacts with the open‑source core of Gewerber.

This Code of Conduct outlines our expectations for participants and describes how to report unacceptable behavior.

---

## 1️⃣ Our Pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in the Gewerber project a **harassment‑free experience** for everyone, regardless of:

- **age**
- **body size**
- **disability**
- **ethnicity**
- **gender identity and expression**
- **level of experience**
- **nationality**
- **personal appearance**
- **race**
- **religion**
- **sexual identity and orientation**

We aim to foster a community where **everyone feels safe, respected, and valued**.

---

## 2️⃣ Our Standards

### ✅ Positive Behaviors We Encourage:
- Being respectful and inclusive
- Offering constructive feedback
- Helping newcomers
- Showing empathy and understanding
- Collaborating openly
- Acknowledging different perspectives
- Focusing on what's best for the community

### ❌ Unacceptable Behaviors Include:
- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Discrimination or exclusion
- Trolling or inflammatory remarks
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing private information without permission
- Threats or violent language
- Disruptive behavior that harms the project or community

If you're unsure whether something is appropriate, **assume it isn't**.

---

## 3️⃣ Responsibilities of Maintainers

Project maintainers are responsible for:

- Clarifying standards of acceptable behavior
- Taking appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior
- Removing or rejecting contributions that violate this Code
- Temporarily or permanently banning offenders when necessary

Maintainers must act **fairly, consistently, and without bias**.

---

## 4️⃣ Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- The Gewerber open‑source repositories
- Issue trackers and discussions
- Pull requests and code reviews
- Community chats and forums
- Public events or online spaces related to the project

It also applies when individuals represent the project officially.

---

## 5️⃣ Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately:

**📧 Email:** `support@gewerber.de`
**📝 Subject:** "Code of Conduct Report"

Include:

- A description of the incident
- Links, screenshots, or context
- Names of involved individuals
- Your preferred level of anonymity

We will respond promptly and confidentially.

---

## 6️⃣ Enforcement

Violations may result in:

- Warning
- Temporary ban
- Permanent ban
- Rejection of contributions
- Removal from community spaces

Actions will be taken at the discretion of project maintainers.
