# Gewerber Brand Book  
**Version 1.0 — Core Identity & Guidelines**

---

## 1. Brand Essence

### Brand Name  
**Gewerber** — a modern, friendly, intelligent platform that helps solo business owners in Germany manage bureaucracy with confidence.

### Tagline  
**Business. Simplified.**

### Core Idea  
Empower individuals to run their business with clarity, confidence, and zero bureaucratic stress.

---

## 2. Brand Values

- **Clarity** — simple, understandable, transparent  
- **Trust** — reliable tools for real business needs  
- **Empowerment** — users feel capable, not overwhelmed  
- **Precision** — German-level accuracy without German-level complexity  
- **Warmth** — friendly tone, human-first design  

---

## 3. Brand Personality

- **Friendly**, not corporate  
- **Smart**, not complicated  
- **Minimalistic**, not sterile  
- **Confident**, not arrogant  
- **Helpful**, not pushy  

### Tone of Voice  
- Clear  
- Calm  
- Supportive  
- Direct  
- Human  

---

## 4. Visual Identity

### Primary Colors

| Purpose | Color Name | Hex |
|--------|------------|-----|
| Primary | Gewerber Blue | `#2D6CDF` |
| Accent | Gewerber Mint | `#4CD4A9` |
| Background | Soft Gray | `#F5F7FA` |
| Text | Deep Slate | `#1F2A33` |
| Error | Red | `#E54848` |
| Success | Green | `#3BB273` |

### Color Philosophy  
- Blue = trust, stability, clarity  
- Mint = freshness, modernity, friendliness  
- Gray = calm, neutral, minimalistic  
- Slate = readable, professional  

---

## 5. Typography

### Primary Font  
**Inter** — modern, readable, perfect for UI.

### Secondary Font  
**Roboto Mono** — for numbers, invoices, and technical data.

### Font Weights  
- Regular (400) — body text  
- Medium (500) — UI labels  
- Semibold (600) — section headers  
- Bold (700) — emphasis  

---

## 6. Logo Guidelines

### Logo Concept  
A clean, geometric wordmark with a subtle “G” symbol representing:

- Guidance  
- Gewerbe  
- Growth  

### Logo Usage Rules

- Minimum padding: 24px  
- Do not stretch or distort  
- Use primary blue on light backgrounds  
- Use white on dark backgrounds  
- Avoid noisy backgrounds  
- Maintain aspect ratio  

### Logo Variants

- Full wordmark  
- Symbol-only “G”  
- Horizontal lockup  
- Monochrome version  

---

---

## 8. UI Design Principles

- **Minimalistic** — no clutter  
- **Accessible** — readable, contrast-friendly  
- **Consistent** — unified spacing, typography, components  
- **Guided** — tooltips, hints, onboarding flows  
- **Calm** — soft colors, predictable interactions  

### Spacing System  
- 4px grid  
- Components follow 8/12/16/24 spacing rules  

### Corner Radius  
- Buttons: 8px  
- Cards: 12px  
- Modals: 16px  

---

## 9. Voice & Tone Guidelines

### Do  
- Be clear  
- Be helpful  
- Be human  
- Be concise  
- Use everyday language  

### Don’t  
- Don’t use bureaucratic jargon  
- Don’t overwhelm with details  
- Don’t sound robotic  
- Don’t blame the user  

### Examples

**Good:**  
“Let’s create your first invoice together.”

**Bad:**  
“Please complete the mandatory invoice creation procedure.”
